Projects

What I own

Atlassian platform on Government Cloud

I led the Jira rollout and now own Jira, Jira Service Management, Confluence, and Assets on Atlassian Government Cloud, across 23 projects. Our tenant runs without marketplace apps, so everything is native config, Automation, and the REST API. The main pieces:

  • Change management process. I designed and launched one path for every system change at the company. Each request carries its scope, the why and the risk, who is affected, a test plan, and a change window, and it passes Director and GRC review gates before anything ships.
  • Application review and onboarding. App access and app change request forms with a section per app, so the requester only sees the questions for what they picked. Requests route to GRC and then Security depending on how risky the app is. A one-click onboarding automation opens the security hardening task and drops a 12-step checklist into the ticket.
  • Other teams' processes. Intake, workflows, and request types for Engineering, Legal, Finance, Infrastructure, and Security, plus an international travel flow for the security office with a work-abroad gate. I wrote a buildout script that turns a manifest into a configured project, so a new team takes hours, not weeks.
  • Identity and hardware. Okta-to-Jira integrations open the onboarding and offboarding tickets automatically, and company hardware is tracked in Assets.

Access model in Okta

I helped write the IAM policies and the Okta group naming convention. Access comes from role-based groups driven by HR attributes, not one-off grants. I also rolled out Okta Access Requests for self-service app access. App access requests to IT are down 76% in the two months since launch.

GitHub identity and access

I own the GitHub IAM build-out in Okta: provisioning from Okta into GitHub, and the team and role groups that decide who gets access to what.

Also in Okta: onboarding and offboarding flows, Okta group to Slack channel sync, Navan SSO and SCIM, the passwordless rollout plan, and helping manage a tenant of 230+ flows.
